Next-Gen Feature Upgrade Blueprint
 SIH 2026 • Problem Statement SIH26133
Accessibility and quality of public healthcare services, particularly in rural and
 underserved areas
PRODUCT DIRECTION
Move from a basic rural-health portal to an intelligent care-access network that connects patients,
frontline workers, doctors, diagnostics, medicines, facilities and emergency services.
DESIGN PRINCIPLE
AI assists. Clinicians decide. The system routes, coordinates, records and follows up.
THE 20-UPGRADE STACK
These features are selected to directly strengthen the core outcomes expected for SIH26133: timely
access, continuity, quality, referral completion, follow-up, diagnostic/medicine visibility, frontline-worker
support, multilingual access, low-connectivity operation, emergency escalation and interoperable records.

KEY DEMO SCENARIO
Scenario: A rural patient reports chest discomfort and breathlessness in a local language. The system
collects structured information, flags the need for urgent evaluation, checks suitable nearby facilities,
shows service capability and availability, initiates emergency/referral workflow, and prepares a concise
clinical handoff. After treatment, the same record powers follow-up and missed-care alerts.
 This single journey can demonstrate AI, multilingual access, rural connectivity, smart
 routing, referral continuity, facility intelligence, emergency escalation and
longitudinal records without turning the product into an AI diagnosis tool.
IMPORTANT PRODUCT GUARDRAILS
• AI outputs should be framed as decision support/triage assistance, not definitive diagnosis.
• Emergency escalation should prioritize human clinical services and verified facility information.
• Health data should use role-based access, consent and auditable sharing.
• Availability data should carry timestamps/status so stale information is not presented as live truth.
• Offline synchronization should resolve conflicts safely and preserve an audit trail.
